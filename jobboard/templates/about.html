WHY HIRELOOP

A clearer way to find your next opportunity.

Technology job listings can be scattered across many sites. HireLoop brings selected public job feeds and direct employer posts into one searchable place, so candidates can compare roles, follow the original listing, and decide what to do next.

YOUR INFORMATION

Private CV matching, on your terms.

The uploaded CV file is discarded after processing. HireLoop keeps extracted text and recognized skills for up to 30 days so it can calculate matches, and you can delete that profile sooner. The CV profile is not shared with employers unless you choose to apply.

If optional AI match explanations are enabled, only the job title and matched or missing skill labels are sent for the explanation—never the CV text.

CLEAR JOB SOURCES

Know where every listing comes from.

Listings are labeled so you can follow the source and check the original details. HireLoop currently includes:

  • RemotiveRemote roles; listings are delayed according to its API terms.
  • RemoteOKRemote job feed with links to the original posting.
  • SkillzDunyaPakistan vacancies, including technology and other categories.
  • Direct employer postsSubmitted to HireLoop and reviewed before publication.

Job details and availability can change at the source. Confirm deadlines, eligibility, and application instructions on the original listing.
