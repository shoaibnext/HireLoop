title: A checklist for comparing software job offers
description: Compare the role, compensation, work arrangement, and written terms before deciding between software job offers.
published: 2026-11-09
updated: 2026-11-09
---

A job offer is easier to evaluate when you compare the same details for each role. Keep the written offer and your questions together so that important terms do not get lost in a conversation.

## Compare the work

Review the title, responsibilities, team, reporting line, technologies, and expected outcomes. Ask what the first months of work are expected to include and how the team will review progress.

## Compare compensation in context

Check the currency, payment schedule, and whether the stated amount is monthly or annual. Clarify how any variable pay is calculated and whether benefits or deductions are described in the written offer. Do not assume two amounts are directly comparable until their terms are clear.

## Compare the work arrangement

Confirm whether the role is remote, hybrid, or office-based; which location rules apply; and what working hours or time-zone overlap are expected. For contract roles, review the contract period, renewal terms, and notice requirements.

## Get open questions answered in writing

Before accepting, ask the employer to clarify any terms that are missing or ambiguous. Keep a copy of the final written offer and make sure it reflects the agreement you intend to accept.
