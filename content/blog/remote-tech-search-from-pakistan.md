Remote work listings can have different location rules. A role marked “remote” may still be limited to a country, time zone, or hiring region. Check the listing details before spending time on an application.

## Start with a clear search

Write down the role names, technologies, and work arrangements you are looking for. A short list makes it easier to compare roles and refine search terms when results are too broad.

On Kaamly.pk, use the skill and location filters to narrow current listings. Open the original posting and confirm that applicants based in Pakistan are eligible.

## Make each application easy to review

Keep a current CV and a small portfolio of work you can discuss. For each role, connect your relevant work to the skills named in the posting. Use examples that you can explain clearly, and avoid adding claims that you cannot support.

## Keep a simple follow-up record

Track the role, company, application date, source link, and any next step. This helps you avoid duplicate applications and prepare for follow-up conversations.

## Review remote-work details

Before accepting an interview or offer, clarify the employer's location requirements, working hours, employment arrangement, equipment expectations, and how compensation will be paid. Ask the employer directly when a listing leaves any of these points open.
